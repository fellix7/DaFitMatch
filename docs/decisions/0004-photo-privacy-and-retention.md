# 0004 — Photo privacy and retention

- **Status:** Accepted (2026-10-15). Cleanup job: Phase 6.

## Context

Try-on needs the user's own photo; the wardrobe feature stores photos of their clothes. These are
personal data and must never leak to other users or be kept longer than needed.

## Decision

- Three **private** buckets, 10 MB limit, image types only: `user-photos` (try-on inputs),
  `tryon-results`, `wardrobe-photos`. Object paths are `{user_id}/<file>`.
- Storage policies: only the owner can read/write their folder. Uploads to `user-photos` and
  `wardrobe-photos` require the age gate (`is_adult`). `tryon-results` are written only by the worker.
- A partner can **read** a wardrobe photo only while that item is part of an outfit shown in a room they
  share; leaving the room removes access.
- Files are served via **short-lived signed URLs** generated by the API; never public URLs.
- **Retention:** unsaved files in `user-photos` older than **30 days** (`PHOTO_RETENTION_DAYS`) are deleted
  by a scheduled pg-boss job (`photos.cleanup`, Phase 6). Wardrobe photos live until the user deletes the
  item or their account (FK cascade).
- The birth date entered at the age gate is used once and **never stored**; only `is_adult` and
  `age_confirmed_at` are kept.

## Consequences

- Any code touching photos needs human review (AGENTS.md §7).
- The try-on cache key is `sha256(user_id || photo bytes)` so caching never crosses users.
